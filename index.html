About glTF

glTF (GL Transmission Format) is a royalty-free 3D file format for efficient transmission and loading of 3D models and scenes. It's often referred to as the "JPEG of 3D" for the web.

How to Use

  • Click the "Load Your glTF File" button to select a .gltf or .glb file from your computer
  • Use your mouse to rotate the model (left-click and drag)
  • Scroll to zoom in and out
  • Right-click and drag to pan the camera

Features

  • Interactive 3D viewer with orbit controls
  • Supports both .gltf (JSON) and .glb (binary) formats
  • Automatic camera positioning and lighting
  • Responsive design for desktop and mobile
